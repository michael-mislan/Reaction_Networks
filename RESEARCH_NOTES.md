# Research notes

This collection studies mathematical models of reaction networks. It includes
formal theorems, conventional mathematical arguments and computational evidence.
Each paper and its accompanying proof notes identify the role of these methods.
Results about a model apply under its stated assumptions; they do not by themselves
establish experimental or clinical conclusions.

The work was developed with AI-agent assistance. Lean verification concerns the
exact formal declarations and their trust assumptions, not the entire manuscript.
The [verification notes](Formal/VERIFICATION.md) describe the available evidence
and reproduction limits. Known editorial corrections appear in the [errata](ERRATA.md).

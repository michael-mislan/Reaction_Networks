# Editorial errata

## P005 — scope of formalization

The abstract, introduction and opening verification overview of
[P005](Formal/papers/P005-raf-realizability/README.md) use wording that is broader than
the detailed qualifications in Section 7.1.

Read those general statements as follows: **the main characterization theorem
and selected corollaries are formalized; Corollary 6.5 (the sharp size bound)
and Theorem 6.6 (the threshold theorem) are proved conventionally and are not
claimed formally verified.** Section 7.1 already states these exceptions.

This corrects the description of evidence, not a mathematical statement or
proof. The original PDF, LaTeX source and Lean files are preserved byte for
byte. Read the PDF together with this correction and its dossier's claim map.

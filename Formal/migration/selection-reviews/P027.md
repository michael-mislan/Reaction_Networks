# P027: published source selection

P027's folder-wide source selection had included an abandoned Mathlib draft
alongside the final literal-certificate development. The
[publication handoff](../../papers/P027-prokaryotic-interventions/evidence/source-maps/00-PUBLICATION_HANDOFF.md)
explicitly identifies the abandoned `ValineSmall` route and distinguishes it
from the warning-free literal certificates used by the manuscript.

The [final publication module](../../proofs/RAFBiochemicalInterventions/PublicationResult.lean)
assembles the literal-source results. The complete transitive closure of all
13 advertised declarations contains 20 modules. Every advertised declaration,
its hypotheses, and every retained source byte is unchanged.

Nine draft-only files were removed from the presentation source tree and
preserved in a private destination archive. Six additional shared dependencies
left P027's closure but remain in the repository for other papers. The
[machine-readable review](P027.json) records both lists, hashes, previous seeds,
retained roots, the full retained closure, and all 13 declaration names.

The draft `SeedCoverage` compilation reported deprecated-tactic and unused
section-variable warnings under warnings-as-errors. Its warning checks were not
disabled. The unnecessary `ValineSmall` invocation was stopped after the handoff
and dependency review established that it belonged to the abandoned route.
These diagnostic results are preserved privately; neither is reported as a
passing production proof. No other paper imports any of the nine excluded files.

This corrects publication selection, rather than altering theorem content or
omitting a failed premise of a published result. The original Erdos files are
untouched. Earlier destination Git commits retain the previous copied selection.
Fresh declaration audits still gate P027's verified status.

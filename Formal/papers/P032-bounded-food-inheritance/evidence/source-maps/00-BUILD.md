# Build notes: finite-count chemical inheritance with reversible interactions and bounded food supplies

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the complete paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one
  inline TikZ figure, three included PDF figures, one `longtable` in Appendix A).
  Author, affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are
  deliberately blank in this version. The companion manuscript is cited through
  `\CompanionAuthor`. Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the breakable
  `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command); do not use it inside math or
  in captions.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 25 entries). All entries are
  primary literature or standard references except `companion2026`
  (the companion manuscript, author macro).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/trajectories.pdf`, `figures/budget.pdf`, `figures/lineage.pdf` —
  vector figures produced by `check_paper.py` (Matplotlib). The trajectory
  figure contains Gillespie sample paths (seed 20260914) and exact
  matrix-exponential curves; the other two evaluate proved formulas.
- `check_paper.py` — replays every constant printed in the paper in exact
  rational arithmetic (generator inequalities on all 81 / 400 / 648 cases,
  the bounds q_9, q_20, q_box, the seventeen-resident ceiling, the sharp gaps
  2/9 and 1/10, detailed balance on every edge, lineage and family
  percentages, the two worked cycles, the tightness margins) and evaluates
  the floating-point diagnostics of Table 1. Writes `check_paper_output.json`.
  Needs numpy, scipy, matplotlib (the repository `.venv` has them).
  Last run (14 September 2026): ALL CHECKS PASSED (47 checks).
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py`, builds in a scratch
  directory, copies the PDF back and to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Finite_Count_Chemical_Inheritance_Bounded_Food.pdf`
(19 pages, 14 September 2026). The last MiKTeX build had no errors, no

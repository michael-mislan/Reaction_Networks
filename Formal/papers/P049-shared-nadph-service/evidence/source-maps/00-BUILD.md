## Files in this folder

  TikZ scheme, three included PDF figures, five tables, an evidence table and a
  claims-to-declarations map in Appendix C). Author, affiliation and date are the
  `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); **author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version**. `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under
  "Reproducibility"; replace it by the public repository location.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 18 entries, all cited, set in
  `\small`). All entries are primary literature or standard references.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `nadph_model.py` — the declared model: parameters, the exact-rational copies,
  the branch quadratics and currents, the full-state reconstruction, the
  eight-variable ODE, the complex-step Jacobian, and the Radau perturbation
  protocol of Appendix A. Nothing in it is fitted.
- `check_paper.py` — exact replay of **every** number printed in the paper
  (106 checks, all passing). Exact-rational `fractions.Fraction` arithmetic
  replays the thresholds `X_G = 3294375/138400918` and `X_T = 460180/489387`, the
  Trx carrier `5000/23009`, the `s_min` enclosure `[0.11371266, 0.11371268]`, the
  redesign root bounds and the design-box scale
  `11621793767394811/92312851976765625`. Floating point reproduces the currents,
  capacities, percentages, the full boundary-state table, the three Jacobian
  spectra, the six transient runs and their minima, the late-time decay rate, the
  orthant-cooperativity pair, and the Section 7 side calculations. Writes
  `check_paper_output.json`. Runtime about 2 minutes (dominated by the six Radau
  integrations to 2000 s).
- `make_figures.py` — the three vector figures. `figures/intersections.pdf`
  (source and demand curves, full range plus boundary detail),
  `figures/quotas.pdf` (required scale versus each quota, with the plateau, the
  switching quota and an inset resolving the narrow interval up to the ceiling),
  `figures/transients.pdf` (service currents over 30 s plus the full-state
  distance to equilibrium over the whole 2000 s integration, log scale, with a
  reference line at the slowest Jacobian eigenvalue).
- `build.ps1` — full chain: `check_paper.py`, `make_figures.py`, a clean LaTeX
  build in a scratch directory under `%TEMP%`, then copies `main.pdf`,
  `main.bbl` and `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent folder.

All three report only the axioms `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`, `propext`.

`Complement.lean` was written for this paper. It closes two gaps that earlier
drafts argued only conventionally:

- **Theorem 3.5 (uniqueness of the full joint steady state).** Previously only
  the scalar NADPH concentration was pinned down formally. `joint_state_unique`
  now proves that two quota-satisfying witnesses agree in every private enzyme
  and carrier coordinate, which is what licenses printing a single reconstructed
  species table (Table 3).
- **Theorem 4.2 (finite-source ceiling).** Earlier work formalized only the
  one-directional obstruction (`Postproof.ceiling_obstruction`). `finite_repair_iff`
  proves the full equivalence: some finite scale admits joint service **iff**
  each quota is strictly below its branch ceiling `j_i(N)`. The sufficiency
  direction needed a new lemma, `ceiling_admits`, deriving all three
  threshold-inversion hypotheses (`p_i - q_i v_i > 0`, `Delta_i > 0`, `X_i < N`)
  from the single inequality `q_i < j_i(N)`, via a strict version of the
  positive-root sign characterization.

The paper's Table 5 classifies every substantive claim as Lean, conventional,
exact rational, or numerical. Do not upgrade a row without checking the actual
declaration interface in the receipt.

## Scope and wording pitfalls

These were all live failure modes in earlier drafts; keep them fixed.

- **Capacity vs realized flux.** `sV` is a capacity parameter; `R_s(x)` is the
  realized current. At the boundary they are `42.642250` and `14.348944` uM/s.
  Never label a capacity as a flux, in text, tables or figure axes.
- **Quotas are design requirements**, not health thresholds. The paper always
  gives the equivalent carrier floor (`y >= 5000/23009`, which is 43.03% of the
  total Trx pool `T` and 50.54% of `S_T = 0.43`) and names the denominator.
- **Removing a branch is not relaxing a quota.** Physical removal deletes the
  demand term from the shared balance; dropping the inequality does not.
- **Overdelivery is relative to the declared quotas**, not biologically wasted.
  The extra peroxide clearance is real.
- **Joint quota failure is not reduced aggregate activity.** At `s_0` the joint
  turnover `12.784739` exceeds the GPx-only current `10.351411` uM/s.
- **Peroxide turnover differs from NADPH consumption** by the repair term
  `(b_T/d_T) j_T`; keep `I_H` and `j_G + j_T` distinct.
- **No global stability claim.** The dynamics section is explicitly numerical,
  and the orthant-cooperativity obstruction (Eq. 32) is included precisely to
  block the tempting monotone-systems shortcut. The apparent 51.55 s crossing at
  `s = s_min` is a sampled observation at zero limiting margin, not a certified
  settling time.
- **No thermodynamic claim.** Forward invariance is a direction-consistency
  check only.
- **The `H = 0.01 uM` clamp is demanding**: `H / I_H` is about `7.0e-4` s. Say so.

## LaTeX pitfalls hit during this build

- `plainnat` routes the `doi` field through `\Url`, which typesets verbatim, so
  an escaped `\_` in a DOI printed as a literal backslash. Fixed by defining
  `\providecommand{\doi}[1]{doi: #1}` in the preamble (it wins over the `.bbl`'s
  own `\providecommand`), which typesets DOIs in text mode.
- `\text{...}` in subscripts was replaced by `\mathrm{...}` throughout, so that
  subscripts stay upright inside italic theorem environments.
- Long Lean identifiers use the breakable `\lean{...}` (`url`-style) command.
  Do **not** use it in captions, section titles or math. The namespace prefix
  `CommonEnvironmentProtection` is too long for running text; it is displayed on
  its own line in Appendix C instead.
- The seven-column coefficient array was 132 pt too wide as an `array`; it is a
  `booktabs` table now.
- `\exp\!\bigl[` pulled the bracket into the operator; drop the `\!`.

### Literal population and the recurrent-mutation red node

The source-driven simulation used actual SSA terminal states, independent recovery, complementary partition, fuel/food refill and retention. No label was reset and no outside-region trajectory was removed by the observer. With two rounds favoring PX and three favoring PY, selected counts were (10,10), (20,11), (40,10), (32,20), (34,40), (33,80). The neutral control ended at (4,16). Total selected-run modeled supply was 27452; neutral supply 13158. No chemical failures occurred in these particular small runs; the implementation nevertheless preserves failures. These are illustrations of selection from standing variation, not empirical proof of uniform adaptation.

The falsifiable hypothesis that a single label-only mutation rate describes mixed founders and pure descendants was tested with 2000 trials at each of (8,2), (10,0), (40,0), in the same F regime. Opposite pairs numbered 17, 0, 0 respectively. Zero of 2000 alone gives a one-sided 95% upper bound about 0.001497, not proof of zero mutation.

Theory gives a stronger structural diagnostic: starting pure, a switch requires a first minority-creating leakage or reverse correction event. Over the deadline, its probability is at most (80*1e-7+64*80^3*5e-11)*(1+1e-7), about 0.001646401. This is below the proven >0.0074 mixed-start switch probability. Thus an exact common label-only switching kernel cannot apply uniformly to these states. Full kernels must retain relevant counts/history, or use justified bounds over a region. Useful recurrent source-level mutation remains RED. The next proof requirement would be a positive, repeated-cycle lower bound from the actual distribution of pure descendants, followed by a finite-population argument. No bulk simulation campaign is warranted by the present evidence.

### Elementary mechanism investigation

One bounded repair-only candidate retained all complexes. For each label it used 2X <-> DX, DX+Y <-> CX, CX+H <-> EX, EX <-> DX+ZX, ZX <-> X+W, and the label-swapped mechanism. All forward model coefficients were 1; reverse dimerization was 1e-4, the next three reverses 1e-5 and the last 1e-4. The complete repair subsystem has 12 species; restoring food, products, replication, production and leakage would give 15 species and 15 reversible pairs. It was not presented as an equivalent implementation of the original source.

Exact stoichiometry conserves resident and fuel moieties. Starting at 8X+1Y+1H, 1000 trials per horizon corrected 0 at 2.5e-9, 0 at 0.1, 168 at 1, and 998 at 10. Mean bound resident moieties were respectively 0, 4.84, 8.599, 8.003. The first dimer's startup probability is bounded above by 56/(4e8)=1.4e-7 at the declared O deadline, already excluding transfer of the fast-startup guarantee for this parameter choice. At long times, chemical correction does not release most residents for the original productive core.

Intact complexes also change partition: 32 free monomers satisfy the >=8-per-daughter condition with probability 2142968775/2147483648; 16 intact dimers carrying the same 32 moieties give only 32071/32768. Equal total resident moieties therefore do not justify the old binomial payoff. This is a quantified obstruction for the tested candidate, not a universal impossibility result. The next door requires a rate/occupancy/partition theorem for a calibrated elementary mechanism. That scientific node remains RED, while the requested feasibility investigation is complete.

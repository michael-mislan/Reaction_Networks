## Corrections made to the draft

- The draft's `\gamma/\beta = 10^{19}` was attributed to "the product of the six
  ratios" of the elementary candidate; it is the product of the **four**
  catalytic turnover ratios (`10^5,10^5,10^5,10^4`) — the dimer `D_X` is
  regenerated by the cycle, so its formation ratio does not enter.
- The `K = 96` comparison certificate is `0.999983…`, not `0.99999…`.
- The separation factor in Corollary 6.3 is 935 against the *certified* switch
  bound; against the rounded threshold `37/5000` it is `924.99991`, i.e. just
  **under** 925, so "over 925-fold" would have been false.
- The coarse "charge all the fuel" comparison is `0.9900657…`, a loss of
  `1.58e-3` (about 2.9× the remaining budget), not `1.8e-3` / "more than three
  times".

# Build notes: productive recovery and repeated harvesting in a reversible autocatalytic reactor

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one TikZ
  reaction-network figure drawn inline, three included PDF figures, three
  tables, one `longtable` claims-to-declarations map in Appendix A). Author,
  affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation
  are deliberately blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default
  `Anonymous`) is the author printed for the five companion manuscripts in the
  bibliography (`thermo2026`, `exporter2026`, `finitecopy2026`,
  `permanence2026`, `inheritance2026`); set it to the author list at the same
  time. `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under "Data and code
  availability"; replace it by the public repository location. Long Lean
  identifiers are typeset with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a
  `url`-style command); do not use it in captions, section titles or math.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 31 entries, all cited; set in `\small`). Entries other
  than the five companion manuscripts are primary literature or standard
  references; DOIs are printed by `plainnat`. The Ameta et al. (2021) and
  Lakshmikantham–Bainov–Simeonov (1989) DOIs were checked online on
  15 September 2026; the others are standard.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/recovery.pdf`, `figures/cycles.pdf`, `figures/retention.pdf` —
  vector figures produced by `make_figures.py` from
  `figures/postproof_pilot.json`, the saved output of the numerical
  diagnostics script `figures/postproof_pilot.py` (SciPy Radau, rtol 1e-9,
  atol 1e-12, no clipping; 192 recovery trials, 32 repeated cycles, about
  42 s). Both are copied unchanged from the campaign workspace. The
  reaction-network figure is TikZ in `main.tex`.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay (run with the repository
  `.venv` Python; needs sympy, mpmath, numpy). Re-derives symbolically the
  material and inventory identities, the exact weighted drift, the elemental
  balance and the common thermochemistry of the six pairs, the coefficient
  comparisons, the parameterized-surplus identity of Lemma 4.6 with its
  explicit nonnegative witness, both slack vectors, the pulse-image extremes,
  every Taylor lower bound (e^3, e^{36/5}, e^{3/2}, e^{12/5}, e^{8/5}) against
  its rational target and against mpmath, the phase-comparison coefficients,
  the transfer coefficients at h = 1/35, the service and food budgets, the
  net-synthesis threshold m = 31, the example state, the entry-band margins,
  the dimensional table, and every entry of the diagnostics table from the
  saved JSON. Writes `check_paper_output.json`.
  Last run (15 September 2026): 74 checks, ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py`, builds in a scratch
  directory under `%TEMP%`, copies `main.pdf`, `main.bbl`, `main.log` back
  here and the PDF to the parent folder).

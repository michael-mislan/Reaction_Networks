# Build notes: exact interfaces for modular autocatalytic reaction networks

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, TikZ figures, listings for
  Lean snippets, natbib + hyperref). Author, affiliation and date are the
  three `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`).
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`unsrtnat` style, numeric, citation order).
  DOIs are carried in `note` fields as `\href` links because `unsrtnat`
  does not print a `doi` field.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Exact_Interfaces_Modular_Autocatalytic_Networks.pdf`.

All figures are drawn in TikZ inside `main.tex`; there are no external
graphics files.

## Items hand-proved, not Lean-verified

Flagged in the paper (Remark 5.10 and Section 10): the n-cycle water-filling
law, the SCC condensation rule, the positive-ray limit for partial actuation,
and the full projective bijection onto the critical surface (Lean has
surjectivity plus injectivity under an explicit kernel-ray-uniqueness
hypothesis). Uniqueness of an ordinary core's child-selection matching is an
explicit hypothesis of Theorem 4.7, cited from the source literature.

# Build notes: trustworthy decisions in stochastic amplification assays

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, four
  included PDF figures, seven tables, a claims-to-declarations map in
  Appendix E). Author, affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at
  the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`);
  author and affiliation are deliberately blank in this version.
  `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed at the end of Section 8; replace
  it by the public repository location. Long Lean identifiers are typeset
  with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command); do not
  use it (or `\path`) in captions, section titles or math — a `\path` in a
  caption aborted the first build.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 30 entries, all cited).
  The companion manuscript entry (`companion2026`) has author "Anonymous";
  update it with the author metadata.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `sign_table.tex` — the outward coefficient table of Appendix B, written by
  `check_paper.py` and `\input` by `main.tex`.
- `figures/decision.pdf`, `large_threshold.pdf`, `capacity.pdf`,
  `censoring.pdf` — produced by `make_figures.py` from
  `check_paper_output.json` (and, for Figure 2(b), from
  `large_threshold_output.json`, the saved Laplace-inversion output of the
  companion package `../Finite_Resource_Diagnostic_Windows_arxiv/`, copied
  here; it takes about 1.5 hours to regenerate and is not rerun).
- `check_paper.py` — exact and directed replay of every printed number
  (run with the repository `.venv` Python; needs numpy, scipy, sympy,
  mpmath). 103 named checks: rational uniformization enclosures for the
  threshold-five and threshold-twenty examples, the fixed-rate window and
  the identity repair; the outward sign certificate; the full h >= 10^6
  certificate (gamma Chernoff + Bernstein constants, directed decimal
  enclosures, the joint-rule and reserve bounds); the deadline optimum,
  limit laws and explicit times; block probabilities, selection regression,
  seeded simulations, capacity sets, sample design, plateau limit, signal
  calibration example. Writes `check_paper_output.json`. Last run
  (16 September 2026): 103 checks, ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `make_figures.py` — all four figures.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py` and
  `make_figures.py`, builds in a scratch directory under `E:\tmp`, asserts
  the log has no undefined references, copies `main.pdf`, `main.bbl`,
  `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Trustworthy_Decisions_Stochastic_Amplification_Assays.pdf`
(27 pages, 17 September 2026 date line).

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File build.ps1
```

or by hand:

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
$env:TEMP = 'E:\tmp'; $env:TMP = 'E:\tmp'
..\..\..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe check_paper.py
..\..\..\.venv\Scripts\python.exe make_figures.py
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

Build in a fresh scratch directory on E: (as `build.ps1` does). A full C:
does not stop pdflatex; it silently drops `main.aux` and ships a PDF with
`??` references, so the script greps the log. Page previews for visual QA:
`pdftoppm -r 70 -png main.pdf render/p` (pdftoppm ships with MiKTeX).

## What this version adds beyond the 13-page workspace manuscript

- A worked-decision introduction, main-results-in-words, evidence labels,
  prior-work paragraph and explicit non-claims.
- Theorem 3.2 (deadline optimality for any fixed sequential source) with a
  complete proof, and Corollary 3.3: all-deadline exclusion is
  all-classifier exclusion. This sharpens the certified all-classifier
  bound at threshold five from 5.55% (Lean) to 6.9% (ordinary proof plus
  the Lean-verified separating certificate at t = 5) and upgrades the
  threshold-twenty deadline comparison to an all-classifier statement.
- Proposition 3.5 (label-neutral pipelines cannot repair the obstruction).
- Section 4: the holding-time representation, beta law, reserve and
  headroom limit theorems (consolidated from the companion package), and
  the new Theorem 4.4 for every h >= 10^6 including the identity repair at
  large threshold; its proof (Appendix C) replaces Chebyshev by gamma
  Chernoff and weighted Bernstein bounds, lowering the sufficient threshold
  from 10^8 to 10^6.
- Theorem 5.3 (strict probability ordering and unique inversion of the
  block probability), Proposition 5.4 (general factorized holding
  innovations), Proposition 5.5 (plateau normalization loses capacity).
- Section 6.1 (signal-to-state intervals with a calibration-uncertainty
  example), the bad-bracket variant of Theorem 6.1, and the
  researcher-facing procedure.
- Regenerated figures (four, with the large-threshold panel new) and the
  claims-to-declarations map.

## Files

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one inline
  TikZ process-and-protocol figure, three included PDF figures, three tables,
  one `longtable` claims-to-evidence map in Appendix C). Author, affiliation
  and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default `Anonymous`) is the author
  printed for the four companion manuscripts in `refs.bib` (`thermo2026` = C0,
  `recovery2026` = C1, `finitecopy2026` = C2, `refinement2026`); set it at the
  same time. `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under "Data and code
  availability". Long Lean identifiers use the breakable `\lean{...}` command
  (a `url`-style command); do not use it in captions, section titles or math.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 29 entries, all cited).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `diagnostic_table.tex` — generated by `make_figures.py`; `main.tex` `\input`s
  it, so it must be shipped with the source.
- `figures/certificate.pdf`, `figures/phase.pdf`, `figures/diagnostics.pdf` —
  produced by `make_figures.py`. The first two evaluate proved bounds and
  closed-form quantities; the third integrates the mass-action ODE associated
  with the reaction table and is labelled as a diagnostic in its caption.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay of every printed constant (92 checks;
  exact rational and integer arithmetic, with positive rational Taylor sums for
  every exponential comparison). Writes `check_paper_output.json`.
  Last run (19 September 2026): ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `make_figures.py` — regenerates the three figures, `diagnostic_table.tex` and
  `diagnostics_summary.json`. Needs numpy, scipy and matplotlib.
- `build.ps1` — full chain: `check_paper.py`, `make_figures.py`, a clean
  three-pass LaTeX build in a scratch directory, then copies `main.pdf`,
  `main.bbl` and `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent folder.
- `receipts/` — the seven strict Lean verification receipts for the modules new
  to this paper, copied from the campaign workspace.

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"

bibtex main
```

Run `build.ps1` **without** `2>&1`: in PowerShell 5.1 that redirection turns
MiKTeX's "you have not checked for updates" notice on stderr into a terminating
error. Page previews for visual QA: `pdftoppm -r 68 -png main.pdf qa/p`.

## What this paper adds to the workspace draft

The draft proved the finite-bath mission at the rounded phase rate `1e-10` with
the per-cycle service allowance `floor(V/5)`, and recorded the halved allowance
as an **open** optional node: a stopped-source tail existed
(`SharpService.lean`) but the joint mission conclusion still read `floor(V/5)`.
This paper closes that node and adds a second, independent refinement. Seven
new Lean modules, all strictly verified with warnings-as-errors and no `sorry`:

| Module | Content |
|---|---|
| `SharpCycle.lean` | sharp one-cycle event, strict stopped→physical transfer, `literal_sharp_cycle_bound` |
| `SharpPhase.lean` | `phaseRate = 1839/8750000000000` carried through window, burn-in, occupation, free collection |
| `SharpPhaseCycle.lean` | refined counter budget; `literal_cycle_phase_bound`, `literal_both_cycle_bound` |
| `SharpEnvelope.lean` | envelope, residual absorption from `V ≥ 1e10`, confidence budgets, `sharp_hundred_budget` |
| `SharpGeneral.lean` | phase-refined **mission** for every admitted bath; loaded-bath instance |
| `SharpMission.lean` | sharp history composition, prefix tolerance, `sharp_example_mission` (V = 9.6e10, R = 9.6e12) |
| `SharpResolution.lean` | refined inverse design; `sharp_hundred_halved_bath` (V = 2e11, R = 2e13, failure ≤ 1e-13) |

`SharpService.lean` (pre-existing, unchanged) supplies the correlated gross
envelope and the stopped tail that `SharpCycle.lean` consumes.

Lean: the changed source and coefficient rectangle; the material, stock and
phase inequalities; the pulse tails; the joint counter budget and prepared
cycle; nonexplosion, the renewal identity and the identification of the stopped
device with the chronological process; the literal cycle bound; the
returned-history conditional law, product and linear bounds; trace existence,
the inventory identity and net synthesis; the directed bath inventory,
all-prefix tolerance and inverse design; neighbouring-state local detailed
balance; the metered-food rate agreement and no-stockout inequalities; and
both refinements together with their propagation to the mission, the sizing
corollary and the two certified instances.

Conventional (flagged at each occurrence in the paper): the endpoint bath
free-energy simplifications (Proposition 6.2) and the force-budget inversion
(Corollary 6.3), which are factorial algebra from the compiled potential and
inventory identities; the metered-food common-randomness path coupling
(Proposition 6.4, the inventory and rate-agreement parts are compiled); the
tilt-optimality remark (Remark 5.2); and the amount and rate-unit conversions
of Section 7.3.

Numerical only: the four deterministic mass-action runs of Section 7.4, which
are diagnostics about mechanism, not evidence about the count process. No
simulation at the certified copy scale is used anywhere in the paper.

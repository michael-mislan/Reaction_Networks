# Unistationarity of Type IIℓ autocatalytic cores with positive degradation, and exact multistationarity beyond minimality

[Read the paper](../../../Theory/Kinetics/P006-type-ii-positive-degradation.pdf) · [Claim map](claims.json) · [Build instructions](../../BUILD.md) · [All papers](../../../README.md)

Nandan, Nghe and Unterberger classified the minimal autocatalytic cores of diluted chemical reaction networks into five families and proved that, under strictly positive linear degradation of every species, every classified core has at most one positive stationary state, with one exception: the family of Type IIℓ cores with ℓ > 2 cyclically ordered one-to-many forks.

The full abstract, hypotheses and numbered results are in the manuscript.

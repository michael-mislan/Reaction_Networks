# Equation and source map

## Primary source boundary

Nandan, Nghe, and Unterberger, *Autocatalytic cores in the diluted regime:
classification and properties*, J. Math. Biol. 92 (2026), article 36,
DOI 10.1007/s00285-026-02357-7, Theorem 5.2, proves at-most-one positive
stationary state with strictly nonzero degradation for every core except
`Type II_l`, `l > 2`; the text explicitly says those cases remain open.

Theorem 3.1(ii) defines `Type II_l` as one directed species cycle with ordered,
disjoint back branches and fork reactions
`x_(i_j) -> s_(i_j) x_(i_j+1) + x_(sigma_j)`. It explicitly permits arbitrary
cycle stoichiometries `s_i` and fixes every back-branch coefficient to one.
The supplementary classification S1.6 gives, for `l=3`, the cyclic order
`i1 < sigma2 < i2 < sigma3 < i3 < sigma1`, and states that some intervening
arcs may be empty. The paper's discussion says variants are generated by
inserting single-species steps with the same multiplicity on both sides.

- Unterberger and Nghe, J. Math. Biol. 85 (2022), article 26, supplies the
  diluted dynamical `(Top)` criterion used by the 2026 classification.
- Steel, Hordijk, and Smith, J. Theor. Biol. 332 (2013), 96--107, is the RAF
  bridge cited by the 2026 paper; it does not itself provide mass-action
  unistationarity.

## Notation map

The source cycle fork `x_(i_j) -> s_(i_j)x_(i_j+1)+x_(sigma_j)` maps in the
unit skeleton to `Q_j -> m_j R_j + P_j`. The arc from `R_j` through the next
fork reactant is represented by `R_j <-> P_(j+1) <-> Q_(j+1)`.
`delta_j` is the net fork current. Positive first-order outflow is denoted
`dX * X`.

## Mathlib facilities

The pinned project is Lean 4.30.0/mathlib 4.30.0. The intended local proof
uses `ring`, `field_simp` after nonzero proofs, `positivity`, `linarith`,
`nlinarith`, and explicit polynomial certificates; it does not use `polyrith`
or a custom axiom.

# Build notes: prediction coverage in inherited-state branching models

Paper: *Prediction coverage in inherited-state branching models: an exact
mean-closure counterexample, a repaired count threshold, and the effect of
founder number* (16 September 2026).

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the complete paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref,
  five included PDF figures, one `longtable` in Appendix B). Author,
  affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation
  are deliberately blank in this version. The three macros
  `\LeanRepairExact`, `\LeanRepair`, `\LeanMinimal` hold the formal-status
  entries of Appendix B for the process-level repair theorem. Long Lean
  identifiers are typeset with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a
  `url`-style command); do not use it inside math or in captions.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 30 entries; primary
  literature and standard references, all cited except `feller1968` is cited
  once in the CLT proof).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/distribution.pdf`, `coverage.pdf`, `founders.pdf`, `grid.pdf`,
  `defect.pdf` — vector figures produced by `check_paper.py` (Matplotlib).
- `check_paper.py` — replays every constant printed in the paper. Exact
  section (fractions + sympy): `c`, `L`, `H`, the geometric-series tail,
  `11/343`, the variance bounds and `55/64`, every integrating-factor constant
  of the analytic box, the `(1/mu0)'` identity, the Bernstein identity with all
  twelve coefficients, `int P = 132541/60000`, the Markov endpoint 10, the
  killed pure-birth ODE identities, the sensitive-founder integral, and the
  generator identities `L N = B - D`, `L N^2 = 2N(B-D)+B+D`. Numerical section
  (floating point, DOP853, rtol 2e-12 / atol 2e-14): the witness from the
  closed low-count backward system and from the truncated forward equation
  (caps 32 and 48, overflow retained), the variance-defect identity by
  quadrature, the founder table to F = 100, the 5 x 6 switching grid, the
  positive-rate rows with F = 100 and a cap-40 forward cross-check, and the
  pinned Browning Fig. 8 reference control (imported from the workspace
  script `experiments/browning_count_boundary.py`). Writes
  `check_paper_output.json`. Needs numpy, scipy, sympy, matplotlib (the
  repository `.venv` has them). Last run: ALL CHECKS PASSED (123 checks, ~2 s).
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py`, builds in a scratch
  directory, copies the PDF back and to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to `..\Inherited_State_Prediction_Coverage.pdf`.

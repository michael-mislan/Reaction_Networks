# Localization of D-instability in star interconnections and a sharp bound on synchronized groups

[Read the paper](../../../Theory/D-Stability/P085-localization-star-interconnections.pdf) · [Manuscript](manuscript/main.tex) · [Claim map](claims.json) · [Build instructions](../../BUILD.md)

D-instability in a star interconnection localizes to boundary systems with at most one synchronized group per channel. The paper proves spectral localization, lifting of strict instability, D-semistability equivalence, and sharpness of the group bound.

**Formalization:** The localization and sharpness results, marginal-gap and lumping examples, and boundary-system counting bounds are formalized. Physical interpretation and numerical illustrations are separate from the formal statements.

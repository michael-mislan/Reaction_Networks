# Build notes: certified localization of reaction deletions in autocatalytic networks

This folder holds the editable sources of the arXiv paper that consolidates the
three repository manuscripts formerly labelled "RQC" (RAF Query Compiler):

- `Certified reuse for repeated RAF queries` (workspace `RAF_RQC_query_compilation`),
- `Support Selection for Certified RAF Deletion Queries` (workspace `RAF_RQC_support_selection`),
- `Reaction-deletion robustness in RAF systems` (workspace `RAF_RQC_reaction_criticality`).

The non-standard term "RQC" is not used in the paper. The object is called a
**ranked support witness**, the method **certified localization** of deletion
queries, and the three parts are localization, witness selection, and exact
robustness laws.

Source files:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, TikZ figures, natbib + hyperref).
  Author, affiliation and date are the three `\newcommand` macros at the top.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`unsrtnat` style, numeric, citation order).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `checks.py` — exact rational checks of the small worked examples quoted in
  the paper (the two five-reaction variances, the gateway moments, the two
  curvature values, the 2728 deletion-subset count). Run `python checks.py`.

Compiled output is copied to `..\Certified_Deletion_Localization_RAF.pdf`.

All figures are drawn in TikZ inside `main.tex`; there are no external graphics
files. The PNG figures of the earlier ReportLab-rendered manuscript remain in
`problem_workspaces\RAF_RQC_reaction_criticality\paper\figures\` and are not used.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```


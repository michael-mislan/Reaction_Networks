Source files in this folder:

  one TikZ proof map, two PDF figures). Author, affiliation and date are the
  macros `\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate` at the top of the

  13 September 2026. The companion entry is an `@unpublished` placeholder;
  replace it with the arXiv identifier once available.

  `figures/reliability.pdf` — not used (kept for the supplement).

## Corrections made to the source manuscripts while merging

- Production manuscript, Section 4.3: the stochastic startup floor was
  printed with `u,w >= 1/2716`, and the displayed inequality
  `eps/(2716^2 (1476+1)) - 2 rho/100 > rho` is **false** (left side
  ~1.8e-19 < rho ~3.3e-19). The Lean proof (`PhysicalProductStartup.lean`)
  uses `u,w >= 1/1358 - 2e-5`, for which the floor is ~6.9e-19 > rho. The paper
  states the Lean constants.
- Production manuscript, eq. (26): the integral bound equals 0.2095311015
  exactly; the paper writes `>=` instead of `>`.
- Production manuscript, Section 5.1: the text did not explain why the
  capacity constant is `4*kappa*L^3/k` (a crude count gives `12*kappa`). The
  paper's Lemma 6.1 follows the Lean argument (`FoodUptake.lean`): weight each
  catalytic ligation by the food mass it consumes and bound the food-mass
  concentration by `L`.

# Build notes: kinetic order is invisible to D-cores

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, hyperref). Author, affiliation
  and date are the three `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); they are placeholders
  and must be set before submission.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (plain style; DOI links are carried in `note`
  fields because `plain.bst` does not print `doi`).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.

Compiled output is copied to `..\Kinetic_Order_Invisible_to_D_Cores.pdf`.

## Editing notes

- Lean identifiers are typeset with `\lean{...}`, defined through the `url`
  package (`\DeclareUrlCommand`), so they break at underscores and dots.
  Write underscores literally inside `\lean{}` (no backslash).
- The two Lean theorem statements in Section 9 are `verbatim` blocks in
  ASCII notation (`forall`, `exists`, `and`, `not`).
- Species and reactions are indexed from 0, matching the Lean sources and the
  companion parameter-rich paper.

## arXiv submission

Upload `main.tex`, `refs.bib` and `main.bbl` (arXiv runs pdflatex itself and
uses the `.bbl`). No figures or custom style files are required.

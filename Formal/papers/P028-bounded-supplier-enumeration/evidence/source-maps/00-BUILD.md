# Build notes: complete enumeration of irreducible autocatalytic sets with bounded supplier choice

Compiled output is copied to `..\Supplier_Bounded_Complete_irrRAF_Enumeration.pdf`
(23 pages: 21 pages main text and appendix, 2 pages references, 31 entries).

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, a4paper, amsthm, natbib `plainnat` +
  hyperref, two inline TikZ figures, one external PDF figure, three booktabs
  tables). Author, affiliation and date are the macros `\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate` at the top of the file; `\PaperAuthor`
  and `\PaperAffiliation` are deliberately empty. `\CompanionAuthor`
  ("Anonymous") fills the author field of the companion-manuscript and
  repository citations (`classification2026`, `certification2026`,
  `repository2026` in `refs.bib`); BibTeX needs a non-empty author to sort.
  Long Lean identifiers and paths are typeset with the url-style
  `\lean{...}` command (underscores unescaped; usable in tables, never in a
  `\caption`). Do not define `\Pr`: LaTeX already provides it.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography. Entries shared with the companion classification
  paper were DOI-checked there on 13 September 2026; the entries added here
  (Tarjan 1972, Sharir 1981, Fredkin 1960, Johnson 1974, Lovász 1975,
  Chvátal 1979, Feige 1998, Dinur–Steurer 2014, Courcelle 1990/2009,
  Downey–Fellows 2013, Cygan et al. 2015) carry publisher DOIs from memory
  and should be spot-checked against Crossref before submission. The three
  `\CompanionAuthor` entries are placeholders to replace with arXiv
  identifiers.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `make_figures.py` — regenerates `figures/scaling.pdf` and
  `scaling_table.tex` (Table 3) from the saved 29-run record
  `problem_workspaces/RAF_tractable_irrRAF_enumeration_structural_restrictions/results/postproof_scaling.json`.
  Figures 1 and 2 are TikZ inside `main.tex`.
- `check_paper.py` — replays every number printed in the paper: the saved
  regression/pilot/scaling records, the six Lean receipts (declarations,
  durations, hashes, axioms, dependency counts), and every worked example
  (six-reaction catalogue, cuts, greedy 6 vs OPT 5, 9/64, gate family
  k = 1..4, modules, two-cycles, chains, shared-product example, all
  diagnostic fixtures) via a standard-library exhaustive reference.
  Run it after changing any printed number. Standard library only.
- `build.ps1` — figure/table regeneration, number replay, pdflatex ×3 +
  bibtex, copy of the PDF one level up.
- `rendered/` — 70 dpi page previews used for visual QA.
- `pass1.log`, `pass2.log`, `pass3.log`, `bibtex.log`, `main.log` — last build
  logs (no errors, no overfull boxes, no undefined references or citations).

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
Copy-Item main.pdf ..\Supplier_Bounded_Complete_irrRAF_Enumeration.pdf
```

or simply `powershell -NoProfile -File build.ps1`. Page previews:
`py -c "import fitz; ..."` (PyMuPDF is installed for `py`, not in `.venv`;
matplotlib is in `.venv`).

Alternative single-pass engine (Tectonic bundled with the Codex LaTeX plugin):

```powershell
& "$env:USERPROFILE\.codex\.tmp\bundled-marketplaces\openai-bundled\plugins\latex\bin\tectonic.exe" -X compile --keep-logs --outdir . main.tex
```

## Content decisions

- Structure follows the sibling arXiv papers in this folder: literature
  positioning in the introduction, informal statement of all five results,
  proof idea, full proofs, examples, formal-verification section with
  concordance and receipt tables, computational section, discussion with
  open problems, reproduction appendix.
- Added relative to the 13-page manuscript: the companion P = NP
  classification as motivation; hitting-set / transversal-hypergraph and
  Fredman–Khachiyan framing of destructive cuts; Feige / Dinur–Steurer
  optimality of the greedy factor; the immediate-deletion counterexample
  (S10.3); the producer-choice validation counterexample (S10.2); the
  shared-unused-product example showing the parameter is sufficient not
  necessary (S9.5); the dense zero-excess chain and the pathwidth-7 gate
  family showing supplier excess and treewidth are incomparable (S13.3);
  the MSO translation of original irreducibility; the worked cost/greedy/
  reliability calculation (S9.2); the intermediate Lean receipts; and the
  2,048-candidate / 2,032-duplicate counter from the t = 8 global run.
- The SI "source-version note" (26 vs 29 scaling calls, imported vs
  independent oracle closure) was checked against the local snapshot: the
  local `postproof_checks.py` has its own `closure`, and the local
  `postproof_scaling.json` has 29 rows including the chain family. The paper
  describes the local snapshot.
- Conventional-proof results (cost bound, β monotonicity, components,
  greedy, zero-excess formulas) are labelled as such in Table 1; nothing in
  the Python implementation is claimed as verified.
- Timings in Table 3 are single instrumented runs and are presented as
  mechanism illustrations only; the counters carry the argument.

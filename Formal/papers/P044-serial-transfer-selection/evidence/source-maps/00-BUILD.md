# Build notes: inherited chemical-state selection across serial transfers

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one TikZ
  protocol figure drawn inline, one included PDF figure, six tables, one
  `longtable` claims-to-declarations map in Appendix C). Author, affiliation
  and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation
  are deliberately blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default
  `Anonymous`) is the author printed for the five companion manuscripts in the
  bibliography (`batch2026`, `copying2026`, `thermo2026`, `recovery2026`,
  `inheritance2026`); set it to the author list at the same time.
  `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under "Data and code
  availability". Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the breakable
  `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command); do not use it in captions,
  section titles or math.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 44 entries, all cited).
  Entries other than the five companion manuscripts are primary literature or
  standard references; DOIs are printed by `plainnat`. The Bardenet–Maillard
  (2015) DOI was checked online on 15 September 2026; Jensen (1953) has no
  DOI (volume and pages confirmed from citing literature); the others are
  reused from the checked bibliographies of the companion packages.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/errors.pdf` — produced by `make_figures.py` (mpmath at 60 digits +
  Matplotlib). It evaluates the proved error formulas; nothing is simulated.
- `figures/postproof_evaluator.json` — the 80-digit term-by-term evaluation of
  the error budget for the three candidate instances and the exact eight-cell
  transfer diagnostic, copied unchanged from the campaign workspace
  (`postproof_evaluator.py/json`).
- `figures/receipts.json` — excerpt (verified flag, exit code, SHA-256,
  toolchain, manifest hash, duration, dependency count, axioms, exported
  declarations) of the two strict verification receipts
  `verification/PublicationMain.verify.json` and `verification/Main.verify.json`.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay (run with the repository `.venv`
  Python; needs sympy, mpmath, numpy). Re-derives exactly: the elemental
  balance and potential assignment of the six pairs, the density drift as a
  sum of the thirteen channels, the stationary curve identities and root
  sign changes, the energy coefficient tables against the Lean lists, the
  exact 1/200 and 42 eigenvalue sandwich (Sylvester minors), the quadratic
  remainder of the drift, the affine budget at the source threshold, the
  readiness radii, u for both witnesses, G2 and every printed decimal, the
  rational thresholds 519/24500, 3322/6125 and 519/12250, the expanded
  chemical error against its barrier form, the caps and the N = 2
  counterexample, the odds and deadline exponents, the transfer moments,
  floors and eligibility, the eight-cell transfer table, R(u), the exact
  rational error certificates with the Lean envelopes (2^-128 <= 1e-38,
  2^-64), the two-cycle confidences, all fourteen evaluator terms for the
  three instances, the clock and quota values, the gross allowance, the
  12NM stock bound, the duration, the readout separation and the receipt
  fields. Two numerical sanity checks (Jacobian certificate eigenvalue and
  cubic-remainder ratio on the box) and one grid check of the odds-drift
  scalar inequality are labelled numerical. Writes `check_paper_output.json`.
  Last run (15 September 2026): 90 checks, ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `make_figures.py` — draws `figures/errors.pdf`.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py` and
  `make_figures.py`, builds in a scratch directory under `%TEMP%`, copies
  `main.pdf`, `main.bbl`, `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent
  folder).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Inherited_Chemical_State_Selection_Serial_Transfers.pdf`

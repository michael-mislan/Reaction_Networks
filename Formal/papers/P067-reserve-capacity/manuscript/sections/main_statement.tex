\section{A robust finite-mission guarantee}\label{sec:main}
\begin{theorem}[Delivered eradication with partially filled reserve]\label{thm:main}
Consider the source in Section~\ref{sec:model}, including its one-percent target-rate uncertainty set of Remark~\ref{rem:box}. Let
\begin{equation}\label{eq:box}
 \begin{gathered}
 k_e\in[99/100,101/100],\quad K=400,\quad h_{\min}=200,\\
 \reff\in[1,2],\quad \sigma\in[2,3],\quad
 \nu\in[0,1/200],\quad H_0\in\{278,\ldots,400\}.
 \end{gathered}
\end{equation}
One and the same administration~\eqref{eq:course}, without observing any unknown parameter, obeys every stated limit and satisfies
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mainbounds}
 \Prob(|Z_{120}|>0)<\frac{107}{12500}=0.00856,\qquad
 \Prob(\tau_H\le120)<\frac7{10^6}.
\end{equation}
Consequently
\begin{equation}\label{eq:joint}
 \Prob(Z_{120}=0,\ \tau_H>120)>0.991433.
\end{equation}
The result remains valid when the healthy rates vary predictably while preserving the comparison bounds in~\eqref{eq:healthy} with $\reff\ge1$ and the mortality ceiling established in~\eqref{eq:healthym}.
\end{theorem}

The conclusion consists of two separate marginal guarantees; the number in~\eqref{eq:joint} is their joint consequence, not an extinction probability by itself. The role of the large reserve is explicit and is not a relabelling: the starting count need only be $278$, but the model has $400$ distinct units and a threshold of $200$. The proof does not achieve the improved renewal requirement by changing the time unit or by subdividing the original $20$-unit source; Table~\ref{tab:meaning} records the dimensionless ratios that are actually changed.

Theorem~\ref{thm:main} follows by combining the finite-threshold reserve theorem of Section~\ref{sec:reserve} with the source-specific drift and delivery calculation of Section~\ref{sec:proof}. Neither side of that argument assumes the desired probability bound as an input. Section~\ref{sec:cheap} then shows that the hypotheses on $H_0$ and on $K$ in~\eqref{eq:box} are far from tight: Corollary~\ref{cor:filling} keeps both mission tolerances down to $H_0=209$, and Corollary~\ref{cor:smaller} replaces $K=400$ by $K=232$.

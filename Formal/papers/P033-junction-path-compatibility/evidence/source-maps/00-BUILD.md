# Build notes: exact common-activity compatibility for junction–path assemblies

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, TikZ for the two graph
  figures, natbib + hyperref). Author, affiliation and date are the three
  `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file; author and affiliation are
  deliberately blank.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`unsrtnat` style, numeric, citation order).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `currents.tex` — the exact current table, generated by `make_figures.py`.
- `make_figures.py` — writes `figures/responses.pdf`, `figures/operating.pdf`
  and `currents.tex` from `data/postproof.json` (matplotlib; curves are
  display samples, every printed number is rational).
- `check_paper.py` — exact rational replay of every explicit number printed
  in the paper (standard library only). Run `py check_paper.py`; it prints
  `PASS`. It guards transcription; it is not a proof of any universal claim.
- `data/` — copies of the exact experiment outputs from the campaign
  workspace (`postproof.json`, `solver_pilot.json`,
  `rational_reconstruction.json`, `performance_pilot.json`,
  `path_diagnostics.json`).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Exact_Common_Activity_Compatibility_Junction_Path_Assemblies.pdf`.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
py make_figures.py
py check_paper.py
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

Alternative single-pass engine (Tectonic bundled with the Codex LaTeX plugin):

```powershell
& "$env:USERPROFILE\.codex\.tmp\bundled-marketplaces\openai-bundled\plugins\latex\bin\tectonic.exe" -X compile --keep-logs --outdir . main.tex
```

Page previews for visual QA: `py -c "import fitz"` (PyMuPDF) is available
under the `py` launcher.

## Lean-verified versus conventional

Lean: Lemma 3.1, Theorem 4.2, Theorem 5.1 (merge + source equivalence),
Proposition 4.4 (via `Factors.decreases` and `unit_source_incompatible`),
the rational checker, Propositions 7.1, 8.1, 8.2 (corner lemmas), the food
window, scaling and instantaneous-loss identities, and all example
evaluations. Conventional (stated as such in the text and in Table 3):
Lemma 6.1 (representation), Lemmas 6.2–6.4 and Theorem 6.1 (bit bound,
resting on Basu–Pollack–Roy one-block quantifier elimination as stated in
Basu's survey, Theorem 2.27, which was checked against the arXiv PDF on
14 September 2026), and the LP complexity in Section 7.

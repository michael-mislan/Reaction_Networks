## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

Build in a fresh scratch directory on a drive with free space (C: was
nearly full on 2026-09-14; the first pdflatex invocation in a fresh
directory reported a spurious MiKTeX "did not succeed" and the later passes
were clean):

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

The build of 2026-09-14 produced 20 pages with zero errors, no undefined
references or citations, and three residual overfull boxes below 2.4 pt
(the matrix displays). Every page was rendered and inspected.

## Editing notes

- Lean identifiers are typeset with `\lean{...}` (url package,
  `\DeclareUrlCommand`), so they break at underscores and dots. Write
  underscores literally inside `\lean{}`. Never use `\lean{}` inside math
  mode or inside a caption; use `\texttt{}` there.
- The two Lean theorem statements in Section 10 are a `verbatim` block in
  ASCII notation (`forall`, `exists`, `and`, `not`, `/=`).
- Species and reactions are indexed from 1 in the paper (the Lean sources
  and the companion papers index from 0; the paper says so where it cites
  their lemma numbers).
- Theorem numbers quoted from the companions were read from their compiled
  PDFs: kinetic-order paper Lemma 4.2, Theorem 4.4, Theorem 5.2, Theorem
  6.3, Theorem 7.1, Questions 8.1–8.3; parameter-rich paper Lemma 4.1,
  Theorem 4.7. Blokhuis–Stadler–Vassena labels (Section 2.3, Definition
  S3.10, Conjecture S3.11) were read from the arXiv v2 PDF, which contains
  the supplement. Vassena's Theorem 5.2 is the arXiv numbering; the SIAM
  version (85(3):1046–1066, 2025) is cited in the bibliography.
- Kuznetsov is cited by section (3.4–3.5, 5.4), not by equation number.

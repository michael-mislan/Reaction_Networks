# Build notes: two consecutive productive windows in random polymer reactors

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, letterpaper, amsthm, natbib
  `plainnat` + hyperref, four included PDF figures, seven tables, one
  claims-to-declarations table in Appendix B). Author, affiliation and date
  are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default `Anonymous`) is the
  author printed for the two companion manuscripts in `refs.bib`
  (`structural2026` = the separation-theorem paper in
  `../Structural_Autocatalysis_Productive_Operation_arxiv`, `diffuse2026` =
  the singleton-dominance paper in `../Diffuse_RAFs_Singleton_Dominance_arxiv`);
  set it to the author list at the same time and replace the two
  `@unpublished` placeholders by arXiv identifiers once available.
  `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence under "Data and code availability".
  Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the breakable `\lean{...}` command
  (a `url`-style command); write underscores unescaped inside it and never use
  it in a `\caption`, a section title or math.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography, 30 entries, all cited. Every journal entry
  carries a DOI. The two companion entries are placeholders (see above).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/timeline.pdf`, `startup.pdf`, `matched.pdf`, `exponent.pdf` —
  produced by `make_figures.py`. The first three reproduce the campaign
  figures from the retained data (`data/ode_*.csv`); `exponent.pdf` is new and
  evaluates the closed-form capped shifted Zipf first moment with mpmath.
  No ODE is integrated and no path is simulated by the figure script.
- `data/results.json`, `data/startup_raw.json`, `data/ode_*.csv`,
  `data/productive_census.json` — copied unchanged from the campaign
  workspace `problem_workspaces/RAF_C6_canonical_capstone_emergence_to_function/paper/data`.
  `startup_raw.json` holds the 800 per-path Gillespie records (seeds
  91000–91199 in each of four rows, deadline 1, event cap 100000 never reached).
- `provenance/experiments.py`, `figures.py`, `conditional_panel.py` — the
  campaign scripts that produced the data (copied for provenance; they import
  from the campaign workspace and are not run by the build).
  `provenance/c6_resolution.verify.json` and `publication_bundle.verify.json`
  are the two strict Lean receipts cited in Appendix B.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay of every number printed in the paper
  (run with the repository `.venv` Python; needs sympy and mpmath). It checks
  the catalogue counts and the 224 census by enumeration at n = 4, 5, 6, 8;
  the noise constants, margins and the 24 e^{-cV/n} envelope; the drift
  algebra (23, 25, 117C, the 3/4 and the 4/5 inequalities, the deficit
  derivative), the endpoint constant 54, the window margins 0.2095/0.1595 and
  the threshold 79; the material ratio 1/12000; the hazard 484 eps/3, the
  ceilings and the necessary scale 14 272 222; the closed-form source moments
  against brute-force sums and the incidence table; every asymptotic formula
  of Theorem 4.4 against exact Hurwitz-zeta evaluations at R = 10^9–10^15 and
  n = 400; the six unequal-tolerance thresholds; the finite obstruction
  10^-11.446; the dimensional conversions; the ODE and Gillespie tables from
  the retained records (including the separately tabulated selected-product
  births and births before corridor exit); and the two receipts. Writes
  `check_paper_output.json`. It performs no simulation.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py` and
  `make_figures.py`, builds in a scratch directory under `%TEMP%`, copies
  `main.pdf`, `main.bbl`, `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent
  folder as `Two_Consecutive_Productive_Windows_Random_Polymer_Reactors.pdf`).

## Corrections and choices made while merging

- The workspace manuscript cited the two predecessors as inaccessible
  "repository manuscripts". They are now cited by their arXiv-package titles
  with `\CompanionAuthor` placeholders, and the specific inherited statements
  (Lemma 5.9, the disabled-law bound, the RAF limit) name the companion
  results they come from.
- The "necessary 1.43e7 molecules" wording of the earlier review is not used:
  the paper calls it the count scale V and states the initial molecule count
  6V ≈ 8.6e7 separately.
- The RAF-conditioning ratio P(F | G_n)/S_n → 1/θ is kept because the
  companion limit P(G_n) → θ is a genuine Lean `Tendsto`
  (`source_RAF_probability_tendsto`), not only a positive liminf.
- The endpoint constant 54 is explained: the elementary range of the
  potential between the stock floor and the mass cap is below 51.6, and 54 is
  the rounded constant of the checked lemma.
- The Gillespie table now separates first nonfood, first selected product,
  births before corridor exit and corridor exits, as the supplement asked; the
  values are recomputed from the raw records and agree with the summary.
- The 4/5 sharpening (Remark 5.3) is presented as an optional refinement that
  is not used by any theorem; its identity is verified symbolically.

# Build notes: complex-balanced realizations of a coupled autocatalytic assembly

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, hyperref, 24 pages). Author,
  affiliation and date are the three `\newcommand` macros at the top of the
  file (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and
  affiliation are deliberately blank and must be set before submission.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (plain style; DOI links are carried in `note`
  fields because `plain.bst` does not print `doi`). The companion manuscript
  `FeedbackBistability2026` is an author-less `@unpublished` entry by design;
  BibTeX warns about it (two warnings, harmless). Every other entry was checked
  against arXiv or Crossref on 2026-09-14.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/networks.pdf`, `figures/intervals.pdf`, `figures/region.pdf` — the
  three figures, regenerated by `make_figures.py` (NumPy, Matplotlib). The
  region plot evaluates the exact criterion of Theorem 5.5 on a grid; it is
  not a sampled classification of realizations.
- `check_paper.py` — exact re-verification (SymPy / Fractions) of every
  number printed in the paper: coefficient rows, all 128 certificate slacks
  and both pairings, the closed forms of F_K and F_J, balance and drift of the
  19-edge flux table, the reduced-function identities, the threshold and root
  factorizations, the permanence identities and comparison bounds, the
  full-rank determinant, both rational examples (including the negative
  example's characteristic polynomial and Hurwitz determinants), the slice
  Jacobian, characteristic coefficients, Hurwitz determinants, the entropy
  principal minors at eta = 5, and the fluxes of Figure 1(b). Prints one line
  when everything passes.
- `main.pdf` — compiled output; also copied to
  `..\Disguised_Toric_CAC_Assemblies.pdf`.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

Build in a fresh scratch directory (in-place MiKTeX builds on this machine
have previously produced spurious end-of-run errors):

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

inspected.

## Editing notes

- Lean identifiers are typeset with `\lean{...}` (url package,
  `\DeclareUrlCommand`), so they break at underscores and dots. Write
  underscores literally inside `\lean{}`. Never use `\lean{}` inside math
  mode or inside a caption; use `\texttt{}` there.
- The Lean definitions and theorem statements in Appendix A are `verbatim`
  blocks in ASCII notation (`forall`, `exists`, `and`, `Sum (Fin 8) (Fin n)`).
- Complex order everywhere is (0, A, B+z, z, H, 2z, B, 2A) and coordinate
  order (A, B, z, H), matching the Lean sources.
- The Jacobian entry (3,3) of the slice is -26/5. The earlier workspace draft
  printed -16/5 there; its characteristic coefficients were already correct,
  and `check_paper.py` asserts the printed matrix against SymPy.
- Theorems are numbered by section; the introduction restates the main
  results as Theorems 1.1-1.5 with pointers to the numbered proofs.

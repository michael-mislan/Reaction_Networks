# Build notes: reaction-minimal cusps in planar bimolecular mass-action networks

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, four
  included PDF figures, two Lean listings, one longtable appendix). Author,
  affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation
  are deliberately blank in this version. Long Lean identifiers are typeset
  with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command): write
  underscores unescaped inside it and never use it in a `\caption`. The Lean

  a mapping before using a new one.

## What is Lean-verified and what is not

Section 9.3 of the paper lists the split. Conventional (hand-proved) steps:
the Lyapunov–Schmidt reduction (Proposition 3.1), the necessary-certificate
lemma (Lemma 3.3), the flux-balance and four-reactant lemmas (4.1, 4.2; the
latter via Banaji–Feliu Corollary 4.25), the key invariant (Lemma 2.2), the
stability argument of Corollary 2.6, the continuum-degeneracy computation of
Section 8.4, and the reductions to published results in Corollary 2.5 and the
inheritance remark. Exact computer algebra (not Lean): the 52 corrected-minor
Bezout identities, the independent enumeration counts, key distinctness, the
source-count split, the 31 ray-skeleton / 5 oriented-matroid counts, and the
rational examples. Keep that list in sync with any change to the mathematics.

Theorem numbers cited from the literature (BBH 2024 Theorem 36; Banaji–Feliu
2026 Corollary 4.25; BBH 2025 Theorem 3.2; Banaji 2026 Section 6.3 and labels
B1–B5) are taken from the workspace's 10 September 2026 literature notes and
should be re-checked against the final published versions before submission.

## Differences from the workspace draft

The 13-page workspace draft was rewritten into this 22-page paper: a fuller
introduction and literature context, the dictionary table of cusp conditions,
the corollaries (five sources necessary, reaction minimality, bistability for
every class) stated up front and proved in a consequences section, the
ray-geometry collision example with its explicit continuum degeneracy, the
inheritance remark, the Lean listings of the certificate predicate and public
theorems, a structured account of the formal coverage proof, a formal-versus-
conventional concordance, a discussion of open problems, and a natbib
bibliography of primary literature with DOIs. The ray-compression experiment,
the inheritance theorem and the global-dynamics questions that the workspace
report deliberately left out of the draft are included here only as short
consequences or open problems; the full follow-up material remains in the
workspace for a possible supplemental report.

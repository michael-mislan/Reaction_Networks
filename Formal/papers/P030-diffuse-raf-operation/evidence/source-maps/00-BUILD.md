# Build notes: diffuse RAFs and productive operation (singleton dominance)

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib `plainnat` + hyperref,
  two TikZ figures drawn inline, no external graphics). Author, affiliation and
  date are the macros `\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate` at the
  top of the file; `\CompanionAuthor` fills the author field of the companion
  separation-theorem citation (`companion2026` in `refs.bib`). Long Lean
  identifiers are typeset with the url-style `\lean{...}` command (write
  underscores unescaped inside it; never use it in a `\caption`).
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (23 entries). The companion entry is an
  `@unpublished` placeholder; replace it with the arXiv identifier once
  available. All other entries carry DOIs.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `check_paper.py` — exact-rational replay of every number printed in the
  paper (catalog counts 30/68/36/224/248/192/32, the four envelopes, the
  mixed/outsider/current budgets, the noise constants, the volume-envelope
  inequalities, the O(1/n) rate constants). Run `python check_paper.py`
  after changing any printed number. It performs no simulation.
- `rendered/` — 70 dpi page previews used for visual QA.

Compiled output is copied to `..\Diffuse_RAFs_Singleton_Dominance.pdf`
(23 pages: 18 pages main text, 3 pages appendices, 2 pages references).

## Corrections and choices made while merging

- The draft cited the predecessor as "Productive autocatalysis in random polymer
  networks: a sharp probability law" and the source development separately.
  Both were merged on 13 Sept 2026 into the single companion paper
  `Structural autocatalysis can be common while productive operation is rare`,
  which is what the paper now cites (`companion2026`). The open question is
  the sentence in that paper's Section 3 ("whether this holds for most
  productive environments is not addressed"), not a numbered Remark 3.4.
- The supplement's Proposition S3.1 (rate O(1/n)) is included as Prop. 5.3
  with its constants re-derived: a_n >= 3/2, R_n <= 2^{2n}, R_n^{2/n} <= 16,
  E D^2 <= 96 R_n, q_{2,n} <= 192/R_n.
- The supplement's Lemma S9.1 bound K_m = 2^{m+1} (not the handoff's looser
  2^{3m+7}) is used; the 36 food-input channels are 4 + 16 + 16.
- The lower prefactor (6/pi^2)^6 ~ 0.0505 is stated as a liminf for s_n/p_n and
  for the reliable-environment fraction; the Lean constant is half of that.
- Nothing from the superseded unshifted source pilot, the C16 cycle, or the
  DAG experiments is used or cited.

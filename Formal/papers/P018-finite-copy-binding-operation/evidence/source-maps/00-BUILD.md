# Build notes: finite-copy productive operation in a reversible autocatalytic binding network

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, two TikZ
  figures drawn inline, one included PDF figure). Author, affiliation and date
  are the three `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`). Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the
  breakable `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command).
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat` style, numeric). DOIs are in `doi`
  fields and are printed by `plainnat`. Every entry was checked against a
  primary source (publisher page, PubMed, arXiv or ADS) on 13 September 2026.
  The `companion2026` entry (random polymer networks manuscript) uses the
  `\PaperAuthor` macro as its author.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `fig_ode.pdf` — Figure 3 (mean-field illustration), generated by `fig_ode.py`
  (`py fig_ode.py`; needs numpy, scipy, matplotlib; writes the PDF and
  `fig_ode_summary.txt` with the numbers quoted in Section 9).
- `check_constants.py` — independent check (`py check_constants.py`; needs
  sympy). Re-derives the generator identities of Lemmas 5.2 and 8.1
  symbolically and evaluates every numerical bound in the paper in exact
  rational arithmetic. Last run: ALL CHECKS PASSED.

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Finite_Copy_Productive_Autocatalysis.pdf` (18 pages, 13 September 2026).

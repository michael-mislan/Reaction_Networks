# Build notes: Exact Overlap Partitions and Gateway Scaling in Kauffman RAF Networks

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, TikZ, hyperref). Author,
  affiliation and date are the three `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (currently the placeholder "Anonymous manuscript").
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (plain style).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/gateway_curves.pdf` — Figure 3, generated by `make_figures.py`
  (matplotlib; reads the 111 published RAF frequencies from
  `problem_workspaces/RAF_overlap_corrected_Kauffman_emergence/experiments/source_raf_results.csv`).
  Figures 1 and 2 are inline TikZ.

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Exact_Overlap_Partitions_and_Gateway_Scaling_in_Kauffman_RAF_Networks.pdf`.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
py make_figures.py
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode main.tex
```

Alternative single-pass engine (Tectonic bundled with the Codex LaTeX plugin):

```powershell
& "$env:USERPROFILE\.codex\.tmp\bundled-marketplaces\openai-bundled\plugins\latex\bin\tectonic.exe" -X compile --keep-logs --outdir . main.tex
```

## arXiv submission

Upload `main.tex`, `refs.bib`, `main.bbl` and `figures/gateway_curves.pdf`
(arXiv runs pdflatex itself and uses the `.bbl`). Before submitting: set the
author macros, confirm that `https://github.com/mmislan/Erdos` is public, and
replace the placeholder author of the companion-manuscript entry
`criticalwindow2026` in `refs.bib`.

## Provenance and claim boundary

The mathematical content is drawn from the Lean development in
`E:\Erdos Problems\proofs\OverlapCorrectedRAF` and the workspaces
`problem_workspaces\RAF_overlap_corrected_Kauffman_emergence` (FINAL_HANDOFF.md,
HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md, SOURCE_LOCK.md, experiments/, notes/) and
`problem_workspaces\RAF_overlap_corrected_Kauffman_emergence_postproof`
(HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER.md). Source hashes in Table 2 were recomputed from the
current Lean files on 2026-09-07 and match the receipts in those handoffs;
artefact hashes are quoted from the handoffs.

Two statements in the paper are proved by hand and are **not** in Lean, and
are labelled as such in the text: Proposition 2.1 (closed-form channel count
via the Lyndon–Schützenberger theorem) and Corollary 9.6 (sharp gateway limit
`1 - exp(-34 lambda)`). The 1 September 2026 campaign report deliberately
left the limit as a numerical conjecture because the commuting-factor estimate
was unformalised; the paper supplies the informal proof and keeps the label.
Everything else (exact overlap law, semantic identification, pairwise
insufficiency, non-overlap recovery, 34-channel gateway, gateway cap, 272 f/n
bound, sublinear vanishing, finite-linear obstruction, bulk factorisation) is
Lean-verified.

Numerical values quoted in the text (census table, gateway values at
lambda = 0.03, RAF frequency vs gateway at n = 6 and n = 10, the 96 -> 34
witness collapse) come from `experiments/source_census_n2_n8.json`,
`experiments/gateway_linear_scale.json` and
`experiments/single_solution_audit.json` in the campaign workspace.

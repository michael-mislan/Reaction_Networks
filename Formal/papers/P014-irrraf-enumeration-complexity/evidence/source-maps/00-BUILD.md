# Build notes: the complexity of complete irreducible autocatalytic families

Paper: "The complexity of complete irreducible autocatalytic families:
output-polynomial enumeration of irreducible RAFs if and only if P = NP".
Compiled output is copied to `..\Complexity_of_Complete_Irreducible_Autocatalytic_Families.pdf`
(22 pages: 19 pages main text and appendices, 3 pages references).

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib `plainnat` + hyperref,
  one TikZ figure of the SAT source, three booktabs tables). Author,
  affiliation and date are the macros `\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`,
  `\PaperDate` at the top of the file; `\CompanionAuthor` fills the author
  field of the two companion-manuscript citations (`certification2026`,
  `minraf2026` in `refs.bib`). Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the
  breakable `\lean{...}` command (url package; write underscores unescaped).
- `refs.bib` — bibliography. Every Crossref-registered DOI was checked
  against the Crossref API on 13 September 2026 (arXiv DOIs are DataCite and
  were not checked that way). The two companion entries are `@unpublished`
  placeholders; replace them with arXiv identifiers once available.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `SUPPLEMENTARY_INFORMATION_draft.md` — the longer supplement draft
  (sections S1–S15) from which Sections 7, 9, 10 and Appendix A of the paper
  were condensed. Material not in the paper (exact clause counts beyond
  Appendix A, the executable semantic reference, the bioengineering query
  table, the benchmark design) can be released as a separate supplement.
- `rendered/` — 70 dpi page previews used for visual QA.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
Copy-Item main.pdf ..\Complexity_of_Complete_Irreducible_Autocatalytic_Families.pdf
```

The last build had no errors, no overfull/underfull boxes, and no undefined
references or citations. Page previews: `py -c "import fitz; ..."` (PyMuPDF
is installed for `py`).

## Content decisions

- The paper keeps the full critical proof path of the eight-page draft and
  adds: literature positioning (enumeration complexity, dualization, MUS
  enumeration, verified TMs), the exact count identity
  |I(Q_phi)| = n + #SAT(phi), the worked example, Section 7 (coNP-completeness,
  #P-hardness, polynomial-delay iff P=NP, deletion survival, essential
  reactions, overlap/low-order-deletion non-indicators, positive vs negative
  frontier), Section 9 (model-assisted enumerator, exact solver-call count,
  bounded pilot), Section 10 (interpretation, limits, open problems).
- Section 7 and Proposition 9.1 are conventional proofs from verified finite
  lemmas; the paper says so explicitly. Nothing in the Python pilot is
  claimed as verified.
- The co-W[P] certification result is cited as a separate companion
  manuscript, not derived here.

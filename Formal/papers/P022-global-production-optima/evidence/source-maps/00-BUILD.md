## What is Lean-verified and what is not

Section 9.3 of the paper lists the conventional (hand-proved) steps: the
subcritical-transient-component uniqueness theorem (Theorem 3.8), the
derivative-balance step of Corollary 3.10, the regularity transfer under fixed
equilibrium constants and the concentration corollary, the constrained-capacity
inequality, the recycling closed forms and ODE convergence beyond the compiled
vector-field signs, and the entire rooted counterexample of Section 7. Keep that
list in sync with any change to the mathematics.

Theorem 3.8 (uniqueness with subcritical transient components) is new in this
paper relative to the workspace draft; its proof is in the text and it is not
compiled.

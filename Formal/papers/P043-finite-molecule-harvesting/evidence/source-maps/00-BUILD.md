# Build notes: reliable repeated harvesting in a finite-molecule autocatalytic reactor

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one TikZ
  network-and-protocol figure drawn inline, three included PDF figures, five
  tables, one `longtable` claims-to-declarations map in Appendix D). Author,
  affiliation and date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file
  (`\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation
  are deliberately blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default
  `Anonymous`) is the author printed for the five companion manuscripts in the
  bibliography (`thermo2026` = C0, `recovery2026` = C1, `exporter2026`,
  `finitecopy2026`, `inheritance2026`); set it to the author list at the same
  time. `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under "Data and code
  availability"; replace it by the public repository location. Long Lean
  identifiers are typeset with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a
  `url`-style command); do not use it in captions, section titles or math.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 43 entries, all cited; set
  in `\small`). Entries other than the five companion manuscripts are primary
  literature or standard references; DOIs are printed by `plainnat`.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/error_horizon.pdf`, `figures/phase_weights.pdf`,
  `figures/residual.pdf` — vector figures produced by `make_figures.py`.
  Every curve evaluates a proved bound (the exact error sum `e(V)` in
  log-sum-exp form, the two-term envelope, the single exponential, the
  certified mission length) or a closed-form quantity (the backward
  phase-transport weights in their large-`V` form). Nothing is a simulation.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay (run with the repository `.venv`
  Python; needs sympy and mpmath). Re-derives symbolically the exact
  generator identities `L A = V - A`, `L B = V - B`, the falling-factorial
  stock correction `L Y = V Y(f(N/V)) + r N_X/(5V)`, the synthesis marks and
  washout marks, the four phase-comparison drifts; checks in exact rational
  arithmetic the restart witnesses, the pulse means and the `-1177/10^9`
  exponent, the material band `[31213/32000, 3231/3200]`, the corridor rate
  bound, the barrier and terminal exponents, the Poisson-clock tails, the
  `(17/16)^27 > 101/20` block estimate, the entry and residence exponents,
  the closed form of the backward weights (against the recursion, symbolically
  in `n`), the window coefficients against `1/35`, the phase exponent, the
  template/food/service exponents, the envelope coefficients, the residual
  absorption `4980^6/720 > 10^6 (V0+1)`, the `e^20` Taylor certificate, every
  hundred-cycle count, the net-synthesis thresholds (55 cycles, `13/1120`,
  `13/1750`), the seven ratios, the sizing-table certificates and the
  unit conversions. Writes `check_paper_output.json`.
  Last run (15 September 2026): 132 checks, ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py` and `make_figures.py`,
  builds in a scratch directory under `%TEMP%`, copies `main.pdf`, `main.bbl`,
  `main.log` back here and the PDF to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to

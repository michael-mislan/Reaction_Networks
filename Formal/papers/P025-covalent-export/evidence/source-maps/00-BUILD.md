# Build notes: reliable covalent export under driven cleavage

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one TikZ
  timeline drawn inline, two included PDF figures). Author, affiliation and
  date are the `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default `Anonymous`) is the author
  printed for the companion finite-copy manuscript in the bibliography; set it
  to the author list at the same time. Long Lean identifiers are typeset with
  the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command); do not use it in
  captions.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric). Entries other than
  `predecessor2026` are primary literature; DOIs are printed by `plainnat`.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/reaction_scheme.pdf`, `figures/certificate_bounds.pdf` — vector
  figures produced by `make_figures.py` (Matplotlib + mpmath; imports
  `certificate_evaluator.py` from the same directory). They evaluate proved
  bounds; nothing is simulated.
- `certificate_evaluator.py` — outward-rounded interval evaluation of the full
  error budget (Theorem 3.2, eq. (14)) and the sufficient-copy-scale search
  used in Section 8. Example: `py certificate_evaluator.py --volume 100000000
  --duration 100 --failure 0.1`.
- `check_constants.py` — independent check (`py check_constants.py`; needs
  sympy). Re-derives the drift and quadratic-rate identities of Lemma 5.2, the
  disabled covalent balance of Theorem 6.1, the moiety/mass identities, and
  every numerical constant of the paper in exact rational arithmetic.
  Last run (14 September 2026): ALL CHECKS PASSED.

Compiled output is copied to `..\Reliable_Covalent_Export_Driven_Cleavage.pdf`
(19 pages, 14 September 2026). The last MiKTeX build had no errors, no
overfull/underfull boxes and no undefined references, citations or hyperref
warnings; every page was rendered and inspected.

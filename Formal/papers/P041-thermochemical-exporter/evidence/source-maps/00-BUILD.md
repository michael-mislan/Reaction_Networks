# Build notes: thermochemically consistent realization of a stochastic autocatalytic exporter

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref, one TikZ
  reaction-network figure drawn inline, two included PDF figures, one
  `longtable` declaration map). Author, affiliation and date are the
  `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. `\CompanionAuthor` (default `Anonymous`) is the
  author printed for the three companion manuscripts in the bibliography
  (`exporter2026`, `finitecopy2026`, `batch2026`); set it to the author list
  at the same time. `\RepositoryNote` is the sentence printed under "Data and
  code availability"; replace it by the public repository location. Long Lean
  identifiers are typeset with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a
  `url`-style command); do not use it in captions or section titles.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric). Entries other than the
  three companion manuscripts are primary literature; DOIs are printed by
  `plainnat`. The Remlein–Esposito–Avanzini preprint (arXiv:2604.14848) was
  supplied by the PI direction and is cited as given; it was not re-checked
  against arXiv from this machine.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/bounds.pdf`, `figures/accounting.pdf`, `figures/figure_data.json`
  — produced by `make_figures.py` (Matplotlib + mpmath). Every curve is an
  evaluation of a proved bound; nothing is simulated. Panel (b) of the bounds
  figure bisects the full error budget for the smallest certifying `V`; that
  curve is explanatory and is not one of the interval-certified table rows.
- `check_paper.py` — independent replay (`py check_paper.py`; needs sympy and
  mpmath). Re-derives in exact rational/symbolic arithmetic the material
  balance, the potential assignment, the six equilibrium ratios, the cycle
  kernel and affinity, the six neighbouring-count ratio identities, the seed
  rate and the necessary-scale constants, the productive-state margins, the
  resident completion, and replays every printed decimal of the paper
  including the outward-interval certifications of the three success rows
  and the three disabled bounds. Last run (14 September 2026):
  111 checks, ALL CHECKS PASSED.

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Thermochemically_Consistent_Realization_Autocatalytic_Exporter.pdf`
(22 pages, 14 September 2026). The last MiKTeX build had no errors, no
overfull boxes, no undefined references or citations; two mild underfull

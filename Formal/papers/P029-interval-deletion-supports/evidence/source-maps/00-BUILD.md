# Build notes: an intrinsic interval-deletion characterization of digraph support families

Paper: "An intrinsic interval-deletion characterization of digraph support
families". Compiled output is copied to
`..\Intrinsic_Interval_Deletion_Characterization_Digraph_Support_Families.pdf`
(18 pages: 16 pages main text, 2 pages references). Built 14 September 2026.

Author, affiliation and date are intentionally blank (macros `\PaperAuthor`,
`\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate` at the top of `main.tex`; the companion
paper is cited as "Anonymous" in `refs.bib`, key `companion2026antimatroid`).

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib `plainnat` + hyperref,
  booktabs tables, one `tabbing` pseudocode block). Lean identifiers are typeset
  with the breakable `\lean{...}` command (url package; write underscores
  unescaped; never use it inside `\caption`). DOIs come from the `doi` field of
  `refs.bib` through the `\doi{}` macro defined in the preamble.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (18 entries, 16 cited; `hordijk2017chasing` and
  `yoshikawa2017representation` are available but unused).
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `build.ps1` — pdflatex, bibtex, pdflatex x3, log check, copy of the PDF.
- `check_examples.py` — exhaustive check (all 2^9 predecessor maps on three
  vertices) of the worked examples in Section 3, the local-body example, and
  the realizable-family counts for n <= 4.
- `census3.py` — verifies the Section 11.3 claim that the six non-realizable
  union-closed families on three elements are the relabellings of the
  conjunction family and the local-body family.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell

```

or by hand:

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
```

The last build had no errors, no overfull/underfull boxes and no undefined
references or citations. MiKTeX prints a harmless "you have not checked for
updates" notice on every invocation. Page previews: `py -c "import fitz; ..."`
(PyMuPDF is installed for `py`).

## Content decisions

- Added relative to the five-page manuscript: a full introduction with
  literature positioning; three worked examples (chain with a loop, including
  a dead-end root choice; the three-cycle with its two realizations and the
  greedy failure; the conjunction family rejected by every branch); the
  normalization fixture as Example 4.2; exact roots/length and forced loops;
  the fixed-policy recognizer with the factorial node bound; an NP-membership
  proposition for explicit lists (digraph certificate + NextClosure
  enumeration); the "no closure axioms" corollary from non-closure under
  intersection; interpolation and local-body counterexamples; the Hall
  matching proposition, its direct-family corollary, and the six-coordinate
  insufficiency example; the single-vertex elimination theorem and its
  corollaries; deletion/products and the composition table; the elementary
  CRS criterion, the RAF factorization corollary and the three-reaction
  conjunction system; the formal concordance table; the census; open problems.
- Proved only on paper (stated in Section 11.2): the node bound (11),
  Proposition 7.2 (NP), Corollary 8.2, the cardinality inequality in
  Proposition 8.5, the explicit three-reaction CRS, Examples 8.3, 8.4, 8.7,
  and the worked examples (checked by `check_examples.py`).
- Not included (candidates for a separate supplement): campaign history and
  AGC records, receipt provenance and hash tables, representation-specific
  cost formula (S32) of the SI, the projection-versus-deletion experiment
  details beyond the two-cycle example, and the evidence-level tables.
- Proposition 7.2 and Corollary 8.2 are new to this write-up (not in the SI
  draft); both have complete proofs in the text.

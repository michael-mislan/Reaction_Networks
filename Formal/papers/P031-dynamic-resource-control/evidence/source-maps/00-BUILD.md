# Build notes: permanence and composition control for self-limited consumers coupled to a dynamic resource network

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the complete paper (article class, amsthm, natbib + hyperref,
  four included PDF figures, three tables). Author, affiliation and date are the
  `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. Long Lean identifiers and file names are typeset with
  the breakable `\lean{...}` command (a `url`-style command); do not use it
  inside math or in captions.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (`plainnat`, numeric, 26 entries, all primary
  literature or standard references). DOIs are printed by `plainnat`.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `figures/rare_recovery.pdf`, `figures/depleted_composition.pdf`,
  `figures/composition_clock.pdf`, `figures/supply_scaling.pdf` — vector
  figures copied unchanged from the campaign workspace
  (`publication/`), where `publication_experiments.py` produces them together
  with the CSV data and the two exact JSON certificates (about 3 s on one
  BLAS thread).
- `check_paper.py` — replays in exact rational/symbolic arithmetic every
  identity and constant printed in the paper (the reservoir square identity,
  quotient and composition equations, variance identities, supply balance, the
  resident drift constants, the rate-cube budgets for A2/B2, the closed-form
  `c_A`, `c_B` constants, the donor polynomial and its enclosure, the branch
  slope, the operating-state numbers of Section 9, the local-certificate
  bounds, matrix and center of Appendix B) and re-hashes the Lean sources
  against the strict receipts named in Appendix C. Writes
  `check_paper_output.json`. Needs sympy (the repository `.venv` has it).
  Last run (14 September 2026): ALL CHECKS PASSED (97 checks).
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py`, builds in a scratch
  directory, copies the PDF back and to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Permanence_Composition_Control_Self_Limited_Consumers_Dynamic_Reservoir.pdf`
(23 pages, 14 September 2026). The last MiKTeX build had no errors, no
overfull/underfull boxes and no undefined references or citations; every page
was rendered and inspected.

## What this version adds relative to the workspace draft

A literature introduction (competitive exclusion, density-dependent chemostat
coexistence, permanence theory, CRN persistence, RAF context, formal
verification) with an informal statement of all results, a method summary and
explicit non-claims; the results collected in one section (Theorems 3.1–3.5,
Propositions 3.6–3.8) with the proofs following; the negative control
(Proposition 3.6, compiled as `no_individual_limitation_extinction_control`)
promoted from the ledger into the paper; a fuller proof of the spectral
factorization; an expanded discussion with open problems; and the expanded
formal correspondence table (now including the composition consequences and
the negative control). All mathematics is that of the workspace draft; the
identities and constants were re-derived by hand while writing and are
replayed by `check_paper.py`.

## Items hand-proved / conventional (stated as such in the paper)

Theorem 3.3 beyond the pairwise ratio limit (the quantitative estimate (19),
the `c/n` floor, the spectral factorization); Theorem 3.4 (prescribed
proportions); Theorem 3.5 except the pointwise extinction and the necessary
supply bound, which are in the formal B3; Proposition 3.7 (branch, exact
rational enclosure plus implicit function theorem); Proposition 3.8 (local
certificate, exact rational interval arithmetic plus a conventional Lyapunov /
Newton–Kantorovich argument); Appendix A as written (its inequalities are the
ones used by the inherited formal resident modules of the single-consumer
donor proof, `RobustPermanence` / `CoreCouplingGlobal`).

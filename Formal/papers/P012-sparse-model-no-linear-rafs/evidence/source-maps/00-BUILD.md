# Build notes: linear-size RAFs are asymptotically absent in the sparse polymer model

Paper: "Linear-size autocatalytic sets are asymptotically absent in the sparse
binary polymer model". Compiled output is copied to
`..\Linear_Size_RAFs_Asymptotically_Absent_Sparse_Polymer_Model.pdf`
(15 pages: 14 pages main text, 1 page references).

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the paper (article class, amsthm, natbib `plainnat` + hyperref,
  one TikZ figure of the proof architecture, two booktabs tables). Author,
  affiliation and date are the macros `\PaperAuthor`, `\PaperAffiliation`,
  `\PaperDate` at the top of the file. Long Lean identifiers are typeset with
  the breakable `\lean{...}` command (url package; write underscores unescaped;
  do not use it inside `\caption`).
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (12 cited entries plus two unused). Every
  Crossref-registered DOI was checked against the Crossref API on
  14 September 2026.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `rendered/` — 80 dpi page previews used for visual QA.

## Compiling (MiKTeX, user scope, not on PATH)

```powershell
$env:Path = "$env:LOCALAPPDATA\Programs\MiKTeX\miktex\bin\x64;$env:Path"
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
bibtex main
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
pdflatex -interaction=nonstopmode -halt-on-error main.tex
Copy-Item main.pdf ..\Linear_Size_RAFs_Asymptotically_Absent_Sparse_Polymer_Model.pdf
```

The last build had no errors, no overfull/underfull boxes, and no undefined
references or citations. On this MiKTeX a pdflatex pass occasionally aborts
with "did not succeed" (the update-check nag); simply rerun it. Page previews:
`py -c "import fitz; ..."` (PyMuPDF is installed for `py`).

## Content decisions

- The paper keeps the full proof chain of the nine-page draft (Sections 4-7)
  and adds: an introduction with literature positioning and a paraphrase of
  the Hordijk-Steel open question; a direct two-frontier proof of the
  single-molecule self-construction result (Section 8), which in Lean is a
  separate theorem, not a corollary of the bounded-rank theorem; a worked
  encoding example (Example 6.2) showing that the factorial counts
  labellings, not productive orders; the formal-verification section with a
  concordance table and the repair found during formalisation; a discussion
  of scope and open problems (growing intensity, alphabets, power law, exact
  scale of M_n).
- Two inputs are cited, not formalised: Hordijk-Steel Theorem 1 (positive
  RAF probability) and Corollary 2 (RAF with fewer than 2 lambda n^2
  reactions). The Lean `size_window_lower_bound` is only the scalar
  combination. The paper says so in Section 3 and Section 10.
- Not included (candidates for a separate supplement): the discovery
  chronology and hypothesis ledger, the numerical preflight tables, the
  growing-intensity scalar lemmas in `PolynomialIntensityAudit.lean`, the
  alphabet audit, and the AGC accounting.
- Author metadata is a placeholder ("Anonymous manuscript"); set the macros
  before submission.

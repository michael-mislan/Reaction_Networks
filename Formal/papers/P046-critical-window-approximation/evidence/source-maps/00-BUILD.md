# Build notes: Effective approximation, low-intensity bounds, and finite-size effects for autocatalytic emergence in reversible polymer networks

Source files in this folder:

- `main.tex` — the complete paper (article class, amsthm, TikZ, hyperref,
  plain BibTeX style). Author, affiliation and date are the three
  `\newcommand` macros at the top of the file (`\PaperAuthor`,
  `\PaperAffiliation`, `\PaperDate`); author and affiliation are deliberately
  blank in this version. Long Lean identifiers are typeset with the breakable
  `\lean{...}` command; do not use it inside math or in captions. The
  `\leanrefs{}`, `\conventional` and `\mixed{}` macros print the evidence
  status line under each result.
- `refs.bib` — bibliography (plain style, 26 entries). The three companion
  manuscripts (`splitwindow2026`, `quotientwindow2026`, `overlapgateway2026`)
  carry the placeholder author `[Author name to be inserted]`; fill these in
  before submission.
- `main.bbl` — generated by BibTeX; arXiv needs it alongside `main.tex`.
- `check_paper.py` — replays every constant printed in the paper in exact
  rational arithmetic (channel counts against literal enumeration for
  n <= 9, the n = 16 deletion terms 112/24/24/24/30, the gateway and singleton
  census 32/36/248 and 30/34/234, the exhaustive history-budget census to
  depth two, the coefficients C_r, the four certificates of Table 2, the two
  n = 16 examples and their rounded table entries, the seed lengths 4830 and
  21520, the log2 r ~ 4843 scale, the sparse coefficient 712483666919430, the
  normalisation envelopes for n = 4..100, and the rational exp/log enclosures
  of Remark 6.5) and draws `figures/history_bounds.pdf` / `.svg` together with
  `figures/bound_curves.csv`. Writes `check_paper_output.json`. Needs
  matplotlib (the repository `.venv` has it). Last run: ALL CHECKS PASSED.
- `build.ps1` — full build chain (runs `check_paper.py`, builds in a scratch
  directory, copies the PDF back and to the parent folder).

Compiled output is copied to
`..\Critical_Window_Shape_Law_Effective_Approximation.pdf`
(20 pages, 14 September 2026). The last MiKTeX build had no errors, no
overfull or underfull boxes and no undefined references or citations; every
page was rendered and inspected.

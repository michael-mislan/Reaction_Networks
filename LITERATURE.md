## Reading the evidence

Results apply to the stated models, encodings and hypotheses. Conditional complexity consequences retain their assumptions; partial resolutions retain their open cases. Tools and results inherited from prior literature or companion papers are identified in the manuscript and its proof notes. The links identify relevant formal developments, rather than asserting that every paper result is a Lean theorem. See [Verification scope](Formal/VERIFICATION.md).

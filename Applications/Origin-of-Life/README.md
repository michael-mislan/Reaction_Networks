# Origin of Life

[Full catalogue](../../CATALOGUE.md) · [Applications](../README.md) · [Topic index](../../TAGS.md#origin-of-life)

These public papers study mathematical questions relevant to chemical origins: when autocatalytic sets emerge, what supports useful operation, and how chemical states can be copied and selected. Their guarantees concern declared reaction and stochastic models; the papers do not establish an empirical origin-of-life pathway.

- **Emergence and operation:** P002 and P036 give critical-window laws; P016 separates structural autocatalysis from productive operation; P041 makes an exporter thermochemically consistent while identifying inherited operating certificates.
- **Inheritance and selection:** P017 and P032 follow finite-molecule copying; P044 and P077 follow finite-population selection across transfers or repeated rounds.
- **Memory and resources:** P052 treats productive inheritance under load; P074 identifies material and kinetic costs of chemical memory.

These are cross-links to the existing artifacts.
